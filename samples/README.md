# Sample clips

These are the clips Gauge was tested on during the hackathon. None of them was filmed by the author of Gauge. Upload any of them to the live site and ask the question listed to check the result yourself.

## Clips from Wikimedia Commons

Each one was converted to H.264 MP4 for SAM 3.1. Two were also cut or cropped (noted below). Every clip keeps its original licence.

| File | Original on Wikimedia Commons | Author and licence | Question to ask | Result Gauge gave |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| `dl_p60_close.mp4` | same clip, cut and cropped to the pendulum | Rhetos, CC BY-SA 4.0 | "What is the swing period?", then "What value of g does that give? The string is 0.60 m." | T = 1.59 ± 0.03 s, g = 9.4 ± 0.3 m/s² |
| `dl_oscillator.mp4` | [Oscillatory motion acceleration.ogv](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oscillatory_motion_acceleration.ogv) | Pieter Kuiper, attribution licence (see the file page) | "What is the spring's frequency?" | f = 0.432 ± 0.004 Hz |

| `dl_juggling.mp4` | [Senkrechter Wurf nach oben (Steigzeit).webm](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Senkrechter_Wurf_nach_oben_(Steigzeit).webm) | Rhetos, CC BY-SA 4.0 | a ball thrown upwards (not in the demo) | |
